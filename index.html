The team

Rouse Robotics.
Team Inferno.

Our 12 students in grades 9–12 lead the team’s software, hardware, CAD, business, and outreach work. We design, build, and program our robot for the FIRST Tech Challenge BIOBUZZ season.

Our mission is to help students become engineers and business leaders through hands-on technical work, leadership, and service. We help other teams develop and make room for every voice, so future students can build on what we learn.

The RoboRaiders program

Rouse revived its robotics program in the 2025–26 FTC season with Robo Raiders 31707, now Rouse Vortex, and Raiders of the Resistance 31989, now Rouse Inferno. Rouse Riptide 36700 joined this season. The three teams have more than 36 members and work together to build a lasting FIRST program at Rouse.

What is FIRST Tech Challenge?

FIRST (For Inspiration and Recognition of Science and Technology) runs this international high-school robotics program. Students design, build, and program reusable metal robots for alliance-based competition. They practice engineering, troubleshooting, and team decision-making through building, programming, and outreach.
